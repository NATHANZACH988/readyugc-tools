Platform directory

Some "Apply" links below are referral links. If you sign up through one, the publisher of this kit may earn a fee. It costs you nothing, and listings are included on the same criteria either way.

The directory has two parts, because they are two different ways of earning. Part A is paid assignments: a brand pays you a set amount when it approves your video. Part B is programmes that pay only if your content gets views or sales. If you came for paid assignments, work through Part A first.

"No followers needed" means the platform's own page says so. Every listing is included on the same criteria whether or not it pays a referral fee.

Part A. Paid for each approved job

You apply, a brand picks you, you deliver, and you are paid the amount shown on the job (less the platform fee) once the video is approved. No audience is needed to be paid, though some of these want to see samples first.

Part B. Paid only on views or sales

These are not paid assignments. Nobody pays you for making the video. You earn only if your own posts get views, or if people buy through your video. Most need an account with followers, and you can do the work and earn nothing. Treat them as something to try later, alongside Part A. Whenever you post paid or gifted content on your own accounts, say clearly in the post that it is an ad.

Check these before you apply anywhere

  • You are 18 or older and live in a country the platform accepts.
  • You have a PayPal account (or the payout method that platform uses) in your own name.
  • You have read what rights the brand gets to your video and for how long.
